Information and Consent

The Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 67/98 of October 26, hereinafter “LPDP”) and the General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and the Council of April 27, 2016, hereinafter “GDPR”) ensure the protection of individuals with regard to the processing of personal data and the free movement of such data. “Personal data” refers to any information, of any kind and regardless of the medium, including sound and image, relating to an identified or identifiable individual. Therefore, the protection does not apply to data of legal entities.

An individual is considered identifiable if they can be identified, directly or indirectly, particularly by reference to an identification number or one or more specific elements of their physical, physiological, mental, economic, cultural, or social identity (e.g., their name associated with their phone number or email address).

By accepting this Privacy Policy, the User gives their informed, explicit, free, and unequivocal consent for the personal data provided through the devlop.systems website to be included in a data processing activity conducted by Devlop, in accordance with the LPDP and GDPR, including security measures, both organizational and technical.

The User guarantees that the information provided is truthful, accurate, complete, and up to date, and is responsible for any damage or loss, whether direct or indirect, that may result from the violation of this obligation.

If the data provided belongs to a third party, the User guarantees that they have informed the third party of the aspects contained in this document and obtained their consent to provide their data to Devlop for the stated purposes.
